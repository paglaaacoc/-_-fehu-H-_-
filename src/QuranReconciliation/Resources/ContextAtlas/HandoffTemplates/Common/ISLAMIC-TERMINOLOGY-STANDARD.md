# THTRP Islamic / Qur'anic Terminology Standard v1

Standard ID:

`thtrp-islamic-terminology-v1`

## Governing rule

Prefer established Qur'anic/Islamic names and terms in English phonetic/transliterated form when they preserve the canonical Islamic identity or concept better than a Biblical/Anglicized substitute.

Retain ordinary English where it communicates just as precisely.

Do **not** replace every ordinary English word with Arabic merely for decoration.

## Names

Preferred examples:

- Adam (a)
- Nuh (a), not Noah
- Hud (a)
- Salih (a)
- Ibrahim (a), not Abraham
- Lut (a), not Lot
- Isma'il (a), not Ishmael
- Ishaq (a), not Isaac
- Ya'qub (a), not Jacob
- Yusuf (a), not Joseph
- Shu'ayb (a)
- Ayyub (a), not Job
- Musa (a), not Moses
- Harun (a), not Aaron
- Dawud (a), not David
- Sulayman (a), not Solomon
- Ilyas (a), not Elijah
- Yunus (a), not Jonah
- Zakariyya (a), not Zechariah
- Yahya (a), not John
- 'Isa (a), not Jesus
- Maryam (a), not Mary
- Idris (a)
- Fir'awn, not Pharaoh
- Iblis / Shaytan according to the Qur'anic referent
- Ya'juj and Ma'juj, not Gog and Magog
- Bani Isra'il, not Children of Israel / Israelites
- Ahl al-Kitab, not People of the Book where the technical Qur'anic category is intended

`(a)` is the compact UI honorific for `alayhi/alayha al-salam` as appropriate.

Do not add `(a)` to figures whose prophetic status is not established merely for consistency.

## Concepts

Prefer when technically intended:

- Qur'an / Qur'anic
- Ayah / Ayat
- Surah
- Juz
- Qiblah
- Salah
- Sawm
- Zakah
- Hajj
- 'Umrah
- Riba
- Tawhid
- Du'a
- Sujud
- Jannah / Jahannam where the specific eschatological referent is intended
- al-Bayt / al-Masjid al-Haram where that precise sanctuary referent is intended

Keep ordinary English such as covenant, justice, revelation, family, judgment, gratitude, law, warning, mercy, and scripture when it remains accurate.

## Source-fidelity exception

Edition 1 preserves GPT-6 Astra's original `coherence_note` and boundary `reason` verbatim, even where Astra used Biblical/Anglicized names.

The normalized Macro headings are the primary browsing labels.

Future AI handoff prompts require this terminology standard for newly generated rationale as well.
